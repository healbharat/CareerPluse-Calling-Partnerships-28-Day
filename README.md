# CareerPluse — Calling, College Partnerships & Software Sales Internship

**Repository:** Public operations and GitHub Issues tracker • **Duration:** 28 working days • **Team:** one dedicated Calling & Partnership Development Intern, reporting to Founder / assigned Marketing Manager.

**Purpose:** Build legitimate college relationships for [CareerPluse.in](https://careerpluse.in), request a discussion about a potential college collaboration / MoU for students, and identify business prospects needing authorised software / website / application development services from Heal Bharat Services. This is a task plan, **not evidence of contacts, signed MoUs, sales, or payments**.

## 1. Required 28-day goals

| Stream | Minimum target | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| College discovery | **3+ relevant colleges researched by the intern** | Each college is distinct, offers relevant programmes, and has an official verifiable institutional channel |
| College outreach | **3 unique colleges approached** | A personalised call attempt to official professional line or institution-appropriate direct email actually placed/sent and privately logged; distinguish attempted from answered |
| MoU opportunity | **At least 1 genuine college collaboration / MoU discussion sought** | Ask a legitimate TPO, HOD, placement officer or management representative to discuss possible collaboration; ideally obtain a meaningful two-way discussion; **signed MoU is a separate milestone** |
| Business discovery | **No artificial upper limit on relevant prospect research** | Find credible companies with plausible website, web-app, app or business software needs; no random mass contacts |
| Software client outreach | **2 distinct, qualified businesses approached** | A personalised and relevant approved call/email actually made or sent within 28 working days; a paying client is NOT required to count an approach |
| Accountability | **28 honest daily reports + weekly reviews** | Day-wise GitHub Issues with only aggregate public-safe evidence; personally identifiable prospect details in restricted private tracker |

### What counts — and what does not

- **Researched:** found through official or reliable public business/college sources, no contact yet.
- **Approached:** an authorised personalised email sent or a professional call attempt placed; privately log date, channel and outcome. A missed call is NOT a two-way discussion.
- **Replied / conversation:** an actual relevant response or substantive two-way discussion.
- **Meeting requested / booked / held:** three different stages. Record separately.
- **MoU signed:** only a fully approved document executed by authorised institution and company signatories. The intern cannot sign or promise it.
- **Client won:** only after authorised acceptance/contract, and **paying client** requires verified payment. Never report an approach as a sale.

## 4. Work rules and approvals

1. **Founder verifies product and service claims before any outreach:** real CareerPluse features, any live pricing/free trial, college partnership offer, actual software services, official company identity and authorised calling number/SIM.
2. **College calling:** intern finds colleges herself. Use official/publicly listed professional contact channels, polite introduction, permission for a short discussion and an offer to send an approved brochure. Avoid claims of guaranteed placements, certificates, internships, scholarships or signed MoU.
3. **MoU:** request a discussion, not a signature on the call. Only authorised representatives may approve clauses and sign. Proposed scope, student consent/data, branding, costs, programme delivery and renewal/termination require company review.
4. **Software calling:** identify genuine service fit; ask whether the company is interested in discussing site/hosting support, web applications, internal business tools or custom software **only if the services are actually approved**. Never promise a particular price, deadline or capabilities without technical review.
5. **Respectful outreach:** no purchased lists, private contact scraping, indiscriminate calls, spam automation or deceptive claims. Respect opt-outs and calling/contact restrictions. Never record calls without appropriate permission.
6. **Company SIM:** use only for authorised company work; follow company custody and return procedures. Do not use for personal calls, impersonation or transferring confidential contacts to personal accounts.
7. **Security:** public repository contains **no direct phone numbers, emails, prospect names tied to private conversations, WhatsApp screenshots, confidential MoU drafts, invoices, CRM exports, OTPs, passwords or call recordings**.
