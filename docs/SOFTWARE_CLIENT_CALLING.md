# Heal Bharat Services — Software Development Client Calling SOP

## Objective
Identify plausible software development buyers with no artificial upper limit on **relevant research**; approach **at least two distinct qualified companies** within 28 working days through legitimate public business channels.

## Research areas (only if company truly provides them)
Potential website development or redesign, internal portals, custom business software, web apps, workflow systems, ongoing maintenance or support. Check verified service scope and founder approval before making any offer. Do not assume a website/domain has expired unless reliably verified and appropriate to discuss.

## Qualification before contact
Use the official business website or legitimate public business listing; assess industry, whether the proposed service may fit and whether there is a relevant public business contact channel. Avoid scraping, purchased lists and indiscriminate calls. A researched company isn't approached.

## Suggested Marathi professional introduction
> नमस्कार, मी Heal Bharat Services कडून बोलत आहे. आम्ही व्यवसायांच्या गरजेनुसार वेबसाइट किंवा सॉफ्टवेअर डेव्हलपमेंटसंबंधी सेवा देतो. तुमच्या संस्थेला सध्या अशा प्रकारच्या कामाची गरज आहे का, किंवा भविष्यात चर्चा करण्यासाठी योग्य व्यक्ती कोण आहेत? तुम्हाला योग्य वाटल्यास आम्ही अधिकृत कंपनी माहिती शेअर करू शकतो. आम्ही आधी तुमची आवश्यकता समजून घेऊ; किंमत आणि वेळ कंपनीची टीम नंतर निश्चित करेल.

## Suggested English professional introduction
> Hello, I'm contacting you on behalf of Heal Bharat Services. We work on approved software and website development services. I wanted to ask whether your business currently has a website, application or business software requirement. If so, may we arrange a brief discovery conversation with our technical team? We can share our verified company profile; scope and quotation would be confirmed after understanding your requirements.

## Questions if the customer engages
- What problem should the website/software solve?
- Existing systems or new build? Main users? Needed features?
- Desired timeframe, internal approval process, and whether they welcome a technical follow-up?
- Next action / preferred professional business contact channel?

## Stages
Researched → Qualified for outreach → **Approached (actually sent/called)** → Replied → Discovery booked → Discovery held → Proposal → Agreement → Paying customer (verified). Count two approaches only when they are to **two different legitimate businesses**.

## Safety and escalation
No quote, contractual commitment, access to their systems, payment request, promise of delivery, or technical guarantee without founder/technical signoff. No call recordings without consent. Professional opt-out respected. Keep raw contact info and call notes in private authorised CRM only. Public Issue comments show aggregate counts.
