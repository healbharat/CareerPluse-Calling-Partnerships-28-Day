- **Day 7:** 3 colleges researched, 1 college approached, 1 business approached as planning milestones; review actuals and missed causes.
- **Day 14:** 3 distinct colleges approached, 2 relevant businesses approached; do not hide blockers.
- **Day 21:** check whether any real MoU/collaboration discussion happened, separately from requested or booked; evaluate replies to buyer discovery.
- **Day 28:** final independently verified scorecard, handover next actions and company property/SIM as required.
