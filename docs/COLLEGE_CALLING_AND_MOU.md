# CareerPluse — College Calling and MoU Conversation SOP

## College shortlist (3 unique colleges minimum)
- Intern independently finds relevant colleges with Engineering, Computer Science, BCA, BSc CS, Diploma or placement/training programmes.
- Check institutional fit and official website; find an appropriate published professional phone or email (TPO, HOD, training/placement officer or management).
- Record official URL, course fit, public contact route, consent status, attempted date and outcome in PRIVATE CRM.

## Approval before first contact
Founder/Manager provides a verified CareerPluse overview, real functioning demo URL, available product features, any actual pricing/trial policy, official calling identity and permission to pitch a proposed partnership. Never promise placements, mandatory student signups, or pre-agreed terms.

## Suggested Marathi professional introduction
> नमस्कार सर/मॅडम, मी Heal Bharat Services कडून बोलत आहे. आमच्या CareerPluse या विद्यार्थ्यांसाठीच्या learning platform संदर्भात आपल्या कॉलेजच्या Training/Placement विभागाशी दोन मिनिटे बोलता येईल का? विद्यार्थ्यांसाठी योग्य असलेल्या काही learning आणि skill-development उपक्रमांबद्दल माहिती द्यायची आहे. संस्थेची परवानगी असल्यास अधिकृत माहिती ईमेलने पाठवू शकते. पुढील चर्चेसाठी योग्य अधिकारी कोण आहेत?

## Suggested English professional introduction
> Hello, I’m calling on behalf of Heal Bharat Services about CareerPluse, a student learning platform. May I speak with your Training and Placement Officer or a relevant faculty coordinator? We would like to share an approved overview and explore whether a student learning or skills collaboration discussion is relevant to your college. May we email information to your official office contact?

## Discussion and possible MoU
If the college is interested, ask whether a short founder-led meeting can be scheduled to explore a possible Memorandum of Understanding. Key non-binding discussion topics: programme purpose and student benefit, what CareerPluse actually offers, institutional responsibilities, voluntary student participation and data privacy, programme/fee terms if applicable, branding approval, term, review, withdrawal and signatures. **Only authorised representatives review or sign MoUs.**

## College stages
Researched → Approached (call/email actually placed/sent) → Replied → Meeting requested → Meeting confirmed → Meeting held / MoU discussed → Draft reviewed → Signed (only if fully executed). A call not answered is **not** a conversation.

## Follow-up etiquette
Use appropriate working hours and permitted channels. Be polite and personalised. No mass calls, no pressure, stop on opt-out; no more than two unanswered follow-ups. Do not record calls without required permission. Give the manager truthful result counts.

## Metrics
3 independently researched distinct colleges; 3 actually approached within 28 working days; make a genuine effort toward at least one two-way MoU/collaboration discussion (not a guaranteed signed document).
